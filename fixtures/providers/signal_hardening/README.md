# Signal-hardening fixture

This fixture is synthetic, redacted and provider-independent. It exercises the
MemeRadar-owned E11.4–E11.8 analyzer contracts for sustained momentum,
economic move funding, deployer activity, bounded launch footprint and token
lock/unlock evidence.

The numeric values are initial research hypotheses only. They are not
MadeOnSol thresholds or production policy. Production policy remains
configuration-driven, versioned and subject to MemeRadar replay/calibration.

The fixture contains no credentials, wallet keys or live-provider payloads.
It must be consumed through the normalized `Observation` contract; no provider
adapter or MadeOnSol dependency is implied.
